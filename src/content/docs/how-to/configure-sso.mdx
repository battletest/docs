---
title: Set up SSO
description: Connect an OpenID Connect identity provider and verify sign-in.
---

## Register the application

1. Open **Settings → Providers → Identity / SSO → Configure**.
2. Copy the displayed **Redirect URI** into an OpenID Connect application in your identity provider. The URL must match exactly.
3. Obtain the issuer URL, client ID, and client secret. Confirm the issuer's discovery endpoint is reachable from the deployment.

## Configure and verify sign-in

1. Enter the issuer URL, client ID, and client secret. Pasted secrets are encrypted before storage; a blank saved secret retains the current value.
2. Keep local password sign-in available while verifying the connection.
3. Select **Test connection** to check discovery and provider configuration, then **Save connection**.
4. Use **Verify sign-in** from the dialog, or open `/auth/oidc/login` on your deployment. Sign in with a test identity and verify the account and organisation access.
5. Disable local password fallback only after sign-in succeeds and you have an administrator recovery procedure.

Connection testing does not exercise a user's full sign-in flow. This identity connection applies to the entire deployment. See [OIDC settings](/docs/reference/adapters/oidc-identity).

BattleTest can notify you per event, per channel. Configure everything at [Settings → Notifications](https://battletest.dev/settings?tab=notifications). For the full list of events and their default channels, see the [Notification Events reference](/docs/reference/notification-events).

## Channels

| Channel | Where it arrives |
|---------|------------------|
| **Email** | Your account email address |
| **Push** | Browser push notifications (requires granting permission in your browser) |
| **In-app** | The notification bell in the dashboard |

The GitHub PR comment is always posted regardless of these settings. It is the primary output of a PR review, not a notification channel.

## Turn a notification on or off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Notifications tab">
    Go to [Settings → Notifications](https://battletest.dev/settings?tab=notifications). Events are grouped by category (Change Reviews, Live testing, Billing & Plan, Account & Security, Team & Organization, Integrations, Run Rooms), each with a master toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the channel for an event">
    Each event has an email, push, and in-app toggle. Switch the channel on or off for that event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some security- and money-critical events (a password change, an ownership transfer, a failed payment on an account that still has billing records) keep **email locked on**; you can add channels but not remove email. These are marked in the [Notification Events reference](/docs/reference/notification-events).

## Enable browser push

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle push on for any event">
    The first time you enable a push toggle, your browser asks for notification permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    Click **Allow**. If you previously blocked notifications for the BattleTest domain, re-enable them in your browser's site settings first, then toggle push again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Push notifications carry a one-tap action where one helps (**Review & approve** on a paused battletest, **View finding** on a confirmed vulnerability) that deep-links straight to the right page.

## Install BattleTest as an app

BattleTest is a Progressive Web App, so you can install it for a dedicated window and reliable push notifications even when no browser tab is open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open BattleTest in a supported browser">
    Use Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser on desktop, or Safari / Chrome on mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    On desktop, click the **install** icon in the address bar (or the browser menu → **Install BattleTest**). On iOS Safari, use **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant notification permission">
    Open the installed app and enable a push toggle if you haven't already, so alerts arrive even when the app is closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See your notifications in the app

The **notification bell** in the dashboard header shows your in-app feed with an unread count. Open it to read recent notifications, jump to the related page from a notification's action, or mark items as read (individually or all at once).

## Get finding data into your own systems

Per-channel notifications are for humans. To pull finding data into a spreadsheet, a ticket tracker, or your own reporting, export it instead. See [How to export findings](/docs/how-to/export-findings).

## Not receiving emails?

Check your spam folder; the first email from `noreply@battletest.dev` is sometimes filtered. Add the address to your contacts. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/how-to/troubleshooting#im-not-receiving-battletest-email-notifications) if it persists.
